# Template: speech roundup (sample)

What central bankers said this month, theme by theme, in their own words.

| | |
|---|---|
| Length | 900–1,400 English words (5–7 minutes); Korean about 3,000–4,500 characters |
| Figures | 0–1 (optionally a who-spoke-about-what table) |
| Editor gates | themes, publish |

## Shape

1. Header: the headline is the month's main conversation; the dek gives the number of speeches and institutions.
2. Key findings: one sentence per theme.
3. One section per theme (3–5). Each has:
   - a one-sentence summary;
   - two to four speakers with one verified quote each;
   - where they differ.
4. A closing "Also said" list for notable single remarks.
5. No insight box: the roundup reports and does not judge.

Rules: at most one sentence quoted per speech; speakers are named with their role and institution; no policy-signal reading the speaker did not state.
