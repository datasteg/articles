# Template: data brief (sample)

A number that moved, what it means, in three charts or fewer. Written the day a release comes out, or when an indicator crosses a line readers care about.

| | |
|---|---|
| Length | 400–700 English words (2–4 minutes); Korean about 1,500–2,500 characters |
| Figures | 1–3: the headline series first, then one comparison |
| Editor gates | plan, publish |

## Shape

1. Header: the headline is the number and its comparison ("Korea's exports rose 9% in September, the fastest since 2022").
2. Key numbers: two or three tiles: the level, the change, and the last time it was this high or low.
3. Three short sections:
   - **What happened**: the number, its period and its source;
   - **Why it matters**: one mechanism, with 1–3 context documents cited;
   - **What to watch**: the next release date and what would change the picture.
4. No question block, no insight box. Keep the references.

Rules: one release per brief; never a forecast; a revision to an earlier release goes in the first section.
