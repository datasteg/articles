# Template: paper digest (sample)

One new paper: what it asks, what it finds, why it matters. This is Glacier's original newsletter template.

| | |
|---|---|
| Length | 700–1,000 English words (4–5 minutes); Korean about 2,500–3,500 characters |
| Figures | 1–2: the paper's key result, redrawn from its own numbers |
| Editor gates | selection, publish |

## Shape

1. Header: the kicker is the institution and series; the headline is the finding in plain words; the dek gives the authors and why it matters.
2. Key findings: 3 sentences from the paper's verified quotes.
3. Five sections:
   - **The question**;
   - **How they studied it** (data and method in plain words);
   - **What they found**;
   - **What it doesn't show** (the paper's own limits);
   - **Why it matters** (for whom).
4. Our read: optional, and only if the editor agrees at selection.

Rules: the paper is source S1, and every finding cites it. At most three context sources. The digest never claims more than the paper does.
