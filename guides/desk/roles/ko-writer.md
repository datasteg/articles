## For the data desk

The strings are a data note's: its title, dek, findings, paragraphs (profile `article`, 해라체), figure headlines,
titles, notes and labels. `en` is the English with its numbers filled in; keep every number of it. When `ko_now` is
given, it is the Korean to improve with the item's `terms`: change only what the terms require.
